About Me

In 2027 I will join the Business School of Beijing Normal University for a PhD. My research interests are behavioral and experimental economics, labor economics, and energy and environmental economics.

本科期间研究Undergraduate Research

本科期间完成两篇第一作者论文：一篇检验国家重点生态功能区政策的降碳协同效应， 入选第二十六届中国经济学年会，并在第八届 IAEE 亚太会议上作汇报； 另一篇从主观幸福感、阶层认同与社会信任等维度， 考察教育对个体福利与决策的影响，入选第二届中国教育经济学研讨会（AWEEC）并宣读。 During my undergraduate studies I completed two first-author papers. The first tests the carbon-reduction co-benefits of China's National Key Ecological Function Zones policy; it was accepted to the 26th China Economics Annual Conference and presented at the 8th IAEE Asia-Oceania Conference. The second examines how education shapes individual welfare and decision-making along dimensions such as subjective well-being, class identification, and social trust; it was accepted and presented at the 2nd China Conference on Economics of Education (AWEEC).

目前正系统学习行为与实验经济学、劳动经济学与能源与环境经济学 的理论与研究方法。 I am currently studying the theory and methods of behavioral and experimental economics, labor economics, and energy and environmental economics in depth.
